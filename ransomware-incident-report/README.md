# Ransomware Incident Report

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a ransomware attack against a small U.S. healthcare clinic. The objective is to identify how the attack occurred, assess its impact, and recommend security measures to prevent similar incidents.

## Scenario

A small U.S. healthcare clinic experienced a ransomware attack that prevented employees from accessing medical records and other critical files. Employees discovered a ransom note demanding payment in exchange for a decryption key.

The attackers gained access through phishing emails containing malicious attachments. After an employee downloaded the attachment, malware was installed and ransomware encrypted the clinic's files.

## Incident Summary

- **Incident Type:** Ransomware Attack
- **Attack Vector:** Phishing Email
- **Industry:** Healthcare
- **Severity:** Critical

## Business Impact

- Medical records became unavailable.
- Business operations stopped.
- Patient care was disrupted.
- Sensitive data was placed at risk.
- The clinic experienced financial and reputational damage.

## Root Cause

The attack was successful because an employee opened a malicious email attachment that installed malware. The attackers then encrypted critical files across the network.

## Recommendations

- Train employees to recognize phishing emails.
- Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).
- Maintain secure offline backups.
- Keep operating systems and software updated.
- Deploy Endpoint Detection and Response (EDR).
- Implement least privilege access controls.
- Monitor systems using a SIEM solution.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Incident Response
- Ransomware Analysis
- Risk Assessment
- Security Awareness
- Technical Documentation
- Business Continuity Planning

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how phishing attacks can lead to ransomware infections that severely impact business operations. Implementing layered security controls can significantly reduce the likelihood and impact of future attacks.
